Tokens

The BBW :root was authored against the lofi role-token vocabulary from the start, so most role names map straight through. Background is #181818, not pure black — pure black is reserved for the app chrome.

Text on yellow is always black (--on-yellow, aliased to --on-dark). Never white. The grey scale is consolidated into --grey-1 to --grey-7 so shades don't get re-scattered; use those rather than inventing a new dark value. Radius is 6px.
