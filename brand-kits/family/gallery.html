The --dark call. Lofis use --dark for CTAs, pressed controls, badges and tooltips. Family's idiom is black for pressed controls (see the segmented control), gold for selection borders, rose for CTAs and radio dots only. So the kit maps --dark to black, which degrades quietly, and keeps rose scarce. During polish: re-point true CTAs to --accent and selection borders to --selected.

Category accents (--c-candy, --c-violet, --c-lake, --c-sky, --c-sea, --c-lawn, --c-sunshine, --c-sunset) each carry a darker -2 pair for text use. Radius is a single 3px step; the lofi's 10px is deliberately overridden.
